About

I am currently a master’s candidate in Photogrammetry and Remote Sensing at Beijing University of Civil Engineering and Architecture under the supervision of my academic advisor Professor Guo Xian, and I am a member of the research group led by Professor Jiang Jie. I received my bachelor’s degree in Surveying and Mapping Engineering from Suzhou University. I once served in the academic committee and the ISPRS student chapter, and won many awards in national surveying and GIS competitions. My research focuses on remote sensing image interpretation, deep learning-based semantic segmentation and object tracking, including high-precision building extraction, spatiotemporal geo-analysis, and intelligent video monitoring, with solid experience in deep learning model development and deployment for remote sensing applications.

Projects

Intelligent Industrial Vehicle Video Analysis System(2025)
As a core developer, built an AI-based real-time monitoring system for industrial vehicles. Adopted YOLO object detection algorithm and ByteTrack tracking algorithm to realize accurate detection, dynamic tracking and operation state analysis of industrial vehicles; constructed a stable RTSP/IP camera streaming pipeline, optimized the automatic reconnection mechanism of video streams to ensure the continuity of monitoring; completed the Docker containerization packaging and deployment of the system, improved the portability and stability of the project, and guaranteed the efficient operation of the system in industrial scenarios.
GitHub
Geospatial Intelligence Engine(2024)
Participated in the key provincial R&D program "Geospatial Intelligence Engine", responsible for the engineering packaging and deployment of deep learning models; realized the containerized management of remote sensing deep learning models based on Docker/K8s technology, and built an extensible model deployment architecture; designed and implemented the land use change analysis workflow, integrated functions such as remote sensing image interpretation and geospatial analysis, and provided technical support for geospatial intelligent decision-making.
